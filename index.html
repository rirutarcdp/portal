👑 The Founder's Journey

A message from Director Brian Kimathi Muthomi

"I was raised in a struggling family, where our daily life was sustained solely by the hard wages of a quarry worker—a stone-cutter and excavator. I had a deep willingness to work hard and pursue a good career, but due to constant school fee challenges, I found myself out of school the majority of the time. This is not just my story; it is the silent reality for the majority of our African families. That is why I am dedicated to changing lives where I can, shifting away from temporary handouts to build permanent financial security for our parents."

📞 0719271310 ✉️ muthomibrian.rcdp@gmail.com

🎯 Our Strategic Core

👁️ Vision Blueprint

A self-reliant Riruta community free from the cycle of short-term financial dependency and school fees distress.

🚀 Mission Framework

To empower vulnerable citizens through socio-economic mentorship, active table-banking setups, and sustainable business management tracking.
