🎯 Vision & Mission

Vision: A self-reliant Riruta community free from the cycle of short-term financial dependency and school fees distress.

Mission: To empower vulnerable citizens through socio-economic mentorship, active table-banking setups, and sustainable business management tracking.

📜 Core Table-Banking Regulations

  • The Handout Rule: RCDP does not give free financial handouts. Emergency aid is structured alongside a mandatory capacity-building tracker.
  • Savings Multiplier: Verified members can access interest-free school fee advances up to 3X their active savings pool.
  • Collateral Guarantee: Two active registered group members must sign as internal trust guarantors before any transaction disbursement.
